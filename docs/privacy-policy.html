Privacy Policy / Kebijakan Privasi • Last updated: November 28, 2026

English — Privacy Policy

GarageLog (package ID: com.garagelog.app) is an offline-first vehicle service, fuel, and garage stock tracking application. We respect your privacy and designed GarageLog so your personal vehicle data stays on your device.

1. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

  • All vehicles, license plates, odometer readings, fuel fill-ups, service history, sparepart brands, garage inventory, and attached receipt photos are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not operate a backend server and do not collect, upload, or sell your vehicle logs or receipt photos.
  • You may export or restore your data at any time using the offline JSON Backup feature. Uninstalling the app deletes locally stored data.

2. Camera, Photos & On-Device OCR

GarageLog requests Camera and Photo Library access only when you choose to attach or scan a workshop receipt. Text recognition is performed 100% on-device using Google ML Kit; your photos are never uploaded to our servers.

3. Local Notifications

Notification permission is used solely to deliver offline local reminders for your upcoming vehicle tax (STNK) and maintenance schedules.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version of GarageLog displays banner and native advertisements powered by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and diagnostic data to serve and measure ads in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. Users who unlock GarageLog Pro Pass receive an ad-free experience.

5. In-App Purchases (Google Play Billing)

Optional one-time purchases (GarageLog Pro Pass) are processed securely through Google Play Billing or Apple App Store. GarageLog does not receive or store your credit card or banking credentials.

Bahasa Indonesia — Kebijakan Privasi

GarageLog (com.garagelog.app) adalah aplikasi pelacak servis, BBM, dan stok garasi yang dirancang mengutamakan privasi dengan penyimpanan lokal di perangkat Anda.

  • Data Lokal: Seluruh data kendaraan, plat nomor, odometer, catatan bensin, riwayat servis, merek sparepart, stok garasi, dan foto nota disimpan langsung di perangkat Anda dan tidak dikirim ke server kami.
  • Kamera & Scan Nota Offline: Akses kamera dan galeri hanya dipakai saat Anda melampirkan atau memindai nota servis. Pembacaan teks (Google ML Kit OCR) berjalan langsung di perangkat Anda.
  • Notifikasi Lokal: Izin notifikasi hanya digunakan untuk pengingat jadwal servis dan pajak STNK secara offline.
  • Iklan (Google AdMob): Versi gratis menampilkan iklan Banner dan Native melalui Google AdMob yang dapat menggunakan Advertising ID sesuai Kebijakan Privasi Google. Pemilik Pro Pass bebas dari semua iklan.
  • Pembelian In-App: Transaksi Pro Pass diproses aman oleh Google Play Billing / Apple App Store.
